# Incident severity

| Sev | Definition | Response | Updates |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Wrong numbers in an executive, financial or regulatory product, or a decision about to be made on bad data | Immediate, incident lead named, stakeholders told within 30 minutes | Every hour |
| 2 | Data product late or partially wrong, workaround exists | Same business day | Twice a day |
| 3 | Minor or internal-only issue | Normal backlog | On close |

Escalate one level when the impact window includes month-end close, payroll,
board reporting or an external filing.
